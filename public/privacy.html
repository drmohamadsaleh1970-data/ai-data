Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026 · AI-DATA / AI Group

AI-DATA is an investor intelligence and customer relationship management service operated by Dr. Mohamad Saleh as part of AI Group. This notice covers this portal and its operator-controlled AI-GROUP-BACKUP connection. For privacy questions or requests, contact drmohamadsaleh1970@gmail.com.

1. Information and purposes

The portal processes account identity, contact and organisation details, memberships, permissions, subscription and payment records, property enquiries, investor records and CRM information supplied or imported into the service. These may include contact details, property preferences, transactions, messages, notes and activity history. We use this information to provide access, operate the CRM and subscriptions, route enquiries, support users, maintain security and records, and recover service data.

2. Google sign-in

When you choose Google sign-in, the portal receives your Google account identifier, verified email address and display name to authenticate and maintain your account. Signing in to the portal does not itself grant access to your Google Drive, Gmail or contacts. Google processes information under its own privacy policy.

3. The operator's Google Drive backup connection

AI-GROUP-BACKUP is an administration connection for the operator's authorised Google account. It is separate from ordinary customer sign-in.

The operator authorises rclone to access Google Drive for storing and recovering encrypted backup archives, under the AI-GROUP-BACKUP folder. Backups can contain application databases, storage objects, files and code, including personal information held in those systems. Archive files and their identifiers, names, sizes and related metadata are processed to upload, list, verify and restore backups. A Drive permission displayed by Google may cover wider file access; the intended operational use is backup administration, not browsing or collecting unrelated personal files. Ordinary portal users do not receive access to the operator's Drive or backup credentials.

Backups are processed on the operator-controlled Hetzner server and stored using restic encryption in Hetzner storage and the authorised Google Drive account. OAuth credentials are stored in access-restricted server configuration. Authorised administrators may access data for operation, support, security and recovery; service providers process it to deliver hosting, identity, database, storage and configured payment services. Data may be processed outside your country, depending on the provider.

4. Google data limits

Google user data is used only for the disclosed authentication or backup functions. It is not sold, used for advertising targeting or used to train general-purpose AI models. Any human access is limited to authorised operational, support, security or legal needs. AI-DATA's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5. Cookies, security and retention

The portal uses a session cookie for authentication and browser storage for preferences such as language. Configured integrations may process additional information when you use them. Access controls and encrypted backup archives help protect data, but no system guarantees absolute security. Operational, billing, security and backup records are retained for the relevant service, recovery and recordkeeping purposes. No automatic backup-deletion interval is promised by this notice. Removing a live record or signing out does not immediately remove historical backup copies; they remain protected until the operator retires them.

6. Your choices and requests

You can sign out and revoke Google authorisation in your Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorised access; it does not automatically delete records or archives already stored. To request access, correction, deletion, an objection to processing, or withdrawal of an optional permission, use the contact above. Requests are subject to identity verification, relevant account authority, applicable rights and lawful recordkeeping needs. For agency-managed CRM records, contact the agency as well.

7. Responsible use and updates

Historical property transactions do not establish marketing consent. Organisations importing or contacting people must have the necessary rights and honour opt-outs. This service is intended for professional use. Material changes to data use will be disclosed here and, when required, through renewed consent before the new use.
