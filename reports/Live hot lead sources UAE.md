# Rewire AI-DATA around signed, native lead webhooks

AI-DATA can receive live, consented leads from almost every channel on its list. **Meta (Lead Ads, Messenger and WhatsApp), Google Ads, TikTok, Snapchat, LinkedIn and Property Finder all push new leads to a webhook within seconds.** The platform cannot safely accept them yet. Every one of these sources delivers at least once and sometimes not at all, and keeps leads for backfill for only 30–90 days. Before any connector ships, AI-DATA has to replace SQLite in `/tmp` with a persistent database and put a durable queue behind signed, idempotent webhook routes. The quickest wins need no partner approval. **Google Ads lead-form webhooks** only need an advertiser to paste AI-DATA's URL and a secret key into each form. **Meta Lead Ads on AI-DATA's own Pages** only need a system-user token. Pair both with Web Push and in-app realtime alerts. **WhatsApp Cloud API with Click-to-WhatsApp ads** comes next: it is the hottest lead channel in the UAE and also the cheapest way to alert agents. Connectors to agencies' own Meta, TikTok, Snapchat and LinkedIn accounts, and to Property Finder and Bayut, wait on weeks of approvals. Start that paperwork on day one, and let Zapier or Make keep feeding the existing `POST /api/public/inquiry` until then. The ownership rule is clear. Property Finder's terms make the portal the data controller and forbid mixing its leads into a pool from other platforms. So portal leads, like an agency's own ad and website leads, may only go to that agency's CRM. **The shared AI-DATA Hot pool may only take leads from AI-DATA's own first-party channels, where the consent wording says the person's details will go to a licensed partner brokerage.** The main legal risk is in the follow-up call, not in capturing the lead. Cabinet Resolutions 56 and 57 of 2024 limit marketing calls to 9am–6pm from company-registered numbers. They require recording with notice and screening against the Do Not Call Registry (DNCR), with fines of up to AED 150,000. No source exempts callbacks to inbound enquiries. So the platform should alert agents immediately but schedule calls inside the legal window. Most official developer and government pages could not be fetched during research. Payload field lists, retry policies, several prices and the legal texts therefore rest on search-engine extracts, and they are flagged where they appear and listed in the section before the conclusion.

## Every major ad network now pushes leads within seconds

Every paid channel AI-DATA named now offers a push path. They differ in three ways: what the webhook carries, how it is authenticated, and what approval stands between AI-DATA and other businesses' ad accounts. Some notifications are **"thin"**: Meta and LinkedIn send only IDs, and AI-DATA must fetch the answers with a stored token. Others are **"fat"**: Google sends the answers inline. Thin sources make token management, not HTTP handling, the main thing that keeps breaking. Expired or revoked tokens produce silence rather than errors.

### Meta Lead Ads deliver IDs in seconds and delete answers after 90 days

Meta POSTs a `leadgen` change on the Page object. It is nested in `entry[].changes[]` and holds only `leadgen_id`, `page_id`, `form_id`, `adgroup_id` and a Unix `created_time` ([Meta Leads webhooks](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/webhooks/getting-started/webhooks-for-leadgen/)). The app then calls `GET /{leadgen_id}` with a token holding **`leads_retrieval`** to read the actual answers in `field_data` ([Meta Retrieving Leads](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/guides/lead-ads/retrieving)). Before any of this fires, each Page must be subscribed with `POST /{page-id}/subscribed_apps?subscribed_fields=leadgen`, which needs `pages_manage_metadata` ([Meta quickstart](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/guides/lead-ads/quickstart/webhooks-integration)). Requests are signed in `X-Hub-Signature-256` with an HMAC-SHA256 keyed by the App Secret ([Hookdeck](https://hookdeck.com/webhooks/platforms/guide-to-whatsapp-webhooks-features-and-best-practices)). The HMAC must be computed over the raw bytes as they arrived, because re-serialised JSON will not match ([GitHub PR](https://github.com/Anchit-AI-Hustle/lifecycle-os/pull/84)). In Express, the route therefore needs `express.raw()` rather than `express.json()`. Meta's own testing guide says the notification fires **"a few seconds"** after submission ([Meta Testing & Troubleshooting](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/guides/lead-ads/testing-troubleshooting)). A third-party guide reports that dropped webhooks are never redelivered ([LeadSync](https://leadsync.me/blog/meta-lead-gen-api-guide/)). Leads can be retrieved for only **90 days** ([Meta Business Help](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/734933888443065)). That makes a poller on `/{form_id}/leads`, filtered on `time_created`, mandatory rather than optional. Its rate limit is **200 × 24 × leads created on the Page in the past 90 days** ([Meta Retrieving Leads](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/guides/lead-ads/retrieving)), which is tiny for a brand-new Page, so new tenants should be polled gently.

Access depends on whose Page it is. For **AI-DATA's own Pages**, a business-owned app in Live mode with a system-user token assigned to those Pages is enough. For **agencies' Pages**, AI-DATA has to clear three stages in order. First comes **Business Verification** by a business admin. Next is **Access Verification as a Tech Provider** ([Meta Access Verification](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/development/release/access-verification.md)). Last is **App Review for Advanced Access** to `leads_retrieval`, `pages_manage_metadata`, `pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement`, and either `ads_management` or `pages_manage_ads`, where sources conflict ([Meta Leads webhooks](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/webhooks/getting-started/webhooks-for-leadgen/); [n8n community](https://community.n8n.io/t/how-to-setup-facebook-trigger-fb-app-for-lead-forms/14317)). For that review, Meta wants a screencast of the full Facebook Login for Business flow. Tech Provider status does not replace App Review ([Meta Tech Providers](https://developers.secure.facebook.com/docs/development/release/tech-providers/)). Advanced Access is subject to **Ongoing Review**, and any client can revoke tokens at any time from Business Integrations ([Meta FBL4B](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/facebook-login/facebook-login-for-business)). Even when all of this is in place, an agency that has customised **Leads Access** in Business Suite will silently block real-time delivery to any CRM it has not granted ([AdsUploader](https://adsuploader.com/blog/facebook-lead-ads-crm-integration); [LeadEnforce](https://leadenforce.com/blog/how-to-enable-leads-access-in-meta-business-suite)). One vendor devotes a whole article to webhooks that are "connected but silent" ([Kredoo](https://blog.kredoo.in/meta-lead-ads-webhook-connected-but-silent/)). Other common failures are using a user token instead of a Page token (error #190) and using a system-user token generated before the Page was assigned ([LeadSync](https://leadsync.me/blog/meta-lead-gen-api-guide/)). Meta allows **one webhook endpoint per app** ([n8n docs](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/trigger-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.facebookleadadstrigger/)). AI-DATA's single production endpoint therefore has to fan out internally to every tenant.

API versioning adds one more trap. Graph API **v25.0 shipped 18 Feb 2026** ([Graph API v25.0 changelog](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/changelog/version25.0)). Localized Meta pages list **v26.0 on 29 Jul 2026**, though the English page could not be confirmed, and **Marketing API v24.0 expired on 6 Oct 2026** ([Meta versions page, ja_JP](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/changelog/versions/?locale=ja_JP)). Calls to an expired version are not rejected; they silently run on the next version ([DEV.to](https://dev.to/flarecanary/metas-graph-api-v20-expires-september-24-your-calls-wont-fail-theyll-quietly-start-running-v21-2m2a)). Pin the version in every call and track its sunset date.

Meta's Instant Forms also produce consent and quality evidence that AI-DATA should store rather than assume. "Higher intent" forms add a review screen and optional SMS one-time-passcode phone verification ([AdsUploader](https://adsuploader.com/blog/facebook-instant-form); [ClickyOwl](https://clickyowl.com/meta-instant-forms-lead-quality/)). Custom disclaimer checkboxes return with the lead. The field name `custom_disclaimer_responses` comes from prior knowledge and is unverified. Closing the loop pays off as well. The Conversions API for CRM accepts lead-stage events keyed by `lead_id` (the `leadgen_id`), with `action_source: system_generated` ([Meta Conversion Leads payload spec](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/conversions-api/conversion-leads-integration/payload-specification)). A search extract says Meta's "qualified leads" goal requires a CAPI integration from April 2026, but this is unverified ([MarTech](https://martech.org/meta-announces-lead-gen-product-upgrades-for-q5-yes-you-read-that-right/)). Meta's Housing Special Ad Category is not mandated for UAE-only targeting. It does apply to ad sets reaching the US, Canada or listed European countries, including the UK ([LeadSync](https://leadsync.me/blog/special-ad-category-meta/)), which off-plan investor campaigns often target.

### Click-to-WhatsApp and Messenger ads hand over a conversation, not a form

Click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA) and Click-to-Messenger ads produce **no `leadgen` event**. Instead, the lead is the first inbound message. On WhatsApp, a message that originates from an ad carries a `referral` object with these fields: `source_id` (the ad ID), `source_type`, `headline`, `body`, media URLs, and `ctwa_clid` for sending conversions back. **`ctwa_clid` is omitted for ads shown in WhatsApp Status** ([Meta WhatsApp webhook reference](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/webhooks/reference/messages/text)). Developers report it missing on other placements too ([Meta developer community](https://developers.secure.facebook.com/community/threads/455417233509676/)). One WhatsApp middleware stripped the entire referral block ([Evolution API #2645](https://github.com/evolution-foundation/evolution-api/issues/2645)). On Messenger, the app must subscribe to both `messages` and `messaging_referrals`. The referral carries `source: "ADS"`, `ad_id` and `ads_context_data` ([Meta Messenger messages webhook](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/webhooks/webhook-events/messages); [messaging_referrals](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/webhooks/webhook-events/messaging_referrals)). Such a lead arrives with only a phone number and a profile name, so qualification has to happen inside the chat. If the business replies within 24 hours, a **72-hour window opens in which all messages are free** ([SleekFlow](https://help.sleekflow.io/whatsapp/understanding-click-to-whatsapp-ads-ctwa-and-the-72-hour-free-window)). One vendor page claims the window stretched to 7 days on 28 Sep 2026, but no other source confirms it ([Spurnow](https://www.spurnow.com/es/blogs/whatsapp-free-entry-point-window)).

### Google Ads lead forms need no app approval, only a URL and a key

Google is the fastest source to switch on because there is **nothing for AI-DATA to get approved**. The advertiser expands the form's export options and pastes a webhook URL and a key. **"Send test data"** then confirms the connection ([Google Ads Help 16729613](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16729613?hl=en)). Each lead arrives as a JSON POST containing `lead_id`, `campaign_id`, `form_id`, `adgroup_id`, `creative_id`, `gcl_id`, `lead_submit_time`, `is_test`, `google_key` and a `user_column_data` array of `{column_id, column_name, string_value}` answers ([Google Lead Form Webhook implementation](https://developers.google.com/google-ads/webhook/docs/implementation)). Authentication is only that shared `google_key` in the body, with **no HMAC**. A long random key per tenant (or per form) and a constant-time compare are therefore essential. Google says delivery is **not exactly once** ([implementation, sl locale](https://developers.google.com/google-ads/webhook/docs/implementation?hl=sl)), so dedupe on `lead_id`. The expected reply is `200 {}`, and receivers should ignore unrecognised fields because new ones get added. If the webhook fails, leads can still be downloaded as CSV ([Google Lead Form Webhook recommendations](https://developers.google.com/google-ads/webhook/docs/recommendations)). That download window is reported as the last **30 days**, with data reportedly retained for 60, and sources conflict ([Privyr](https://www.privyr.com/blog/how-to-download-leads-from-google-ads/)). The Google Ads API's `lead_form_submission_data` resource supports nightly reconciliation by GAQL polling ([Google Ads API fields](https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/fields/v21/lead_form_submission_data)). Its developer-token requirement is unverified. To send outcomes back, Google recommends **enhanced conversions for leads** and reports a median 10% conversion lift when first-party data accompanies GCLIDs ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/15713840?hl=en)). One third-party source says these uploads moved to the Data Manager API on 15 Jun 2026 ([Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/google-ads-en/google-ads-offline-conversion-import-guide/)). Lead-form eligibility for UAE real-estate advertisers was not verified.

### TikTok and Snapchat push through OAuth apps; LinkedIn adds its own approval gate

**TikTok** offers four lead routes: native CRM integrations, third-party connectors, Leads Center download, and **"TikTok Custom API with Webhooks"** ([TikTok Help](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/available-crm-integrations-tiktok-lead-generation?lang=en%3D)). The webhook route needs an API for Business developer app. Each advertiser authorises it with OAuth, and AI-DATA then calls `/subscription/subscribe/` with a callback `url` ([TikTok API for Business docs](https://ads.tiktok.com/gateway/docs/index?identify_key=2b9b4278e47b275f36e7c39a4af4ba067d088e031d5f5fe45d381559ac89ba48&language=ENGLISH&doc_id=1701890921224193)). TikTok keeps leads for only **90 days** and lets only Admins download them ([TikTok Help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/access-leads-data-on-instant-forms?lang=en)). The lead webhook's **signature scheme and payload field names were not found**. TikTok's general developer webhooks retry for up to 72 hours ([TikTok for Developers](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/webhooks-overview/)), but that may be a different product line.

**Snapchat**'s Marketing API creates a webhook integration **per form, and only one per form**. It includes a test operation, and the verification scheme uses a timestamp `t` header ([Snap Lead Generation Ads Guide](https://developers.snap.com/marketing-api/Ads-API/lead-generation-ads)). A client already running another webhook tool on that form will therefore conflict with AI-DATA. Snap also has a native HubSpot integration ([Marketing Dive](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/snapchat-shores-up-lead-gen-ads-with-new-hubspot-integration/826552/)).

**LinkedIn** is the hardest of the four. Lead Sync is **a separate program from the Advertising API**, and it requires a verified Company Page ([LinkedIn: getting access](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/lead-sync/getting-access-leadsync)). The permission appears as `r_marketing_leadgen_automation` in older docs and `r_ads_leadgen_automation` in newer ones ([LinkedIn Lead Sync use cases, 2026-10](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/lead-sync/usecases?view=li-lms-2026-10&viewFallbackFrom=li-lms-2025-05)). Subscriptions can only be created through the `leadNotifications` API ([Hookdeck](https://hookdeck.com/webhooks/platforms/guide-to-linkedin-webhooks-features-and-best-practices)). Since **16 Mar 2026, unvalidated webhook URLs receive no new-lead events** ([LinkedIn Lead Syncing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/lead-sync/leadsync?view=li-lms-2026-07)). Validation works like this: within **3 seconds**, AI-DATA must answer a `challengeCode` with its hex HMAC-SHA256 keyed by the client secret. LinkedIn repeats the check about **every two hours**, and event POSTs carry an `X-LI-Signature` header ([LinkedIn webhook validation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/shared/api-guide/webhook-validation)). Legacy `LeadNotificationUrls` subscriptions keep sending, so running old and new side by side produces duplicates ([LinkedIn migration guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/lead-sync/lead-sync-api-migration-guide?view=li-lms-2026-06)).

No lead webhook was found for **Microsoft Advertising**, whose new lead integration targets HubSpot ([Microsoft Advertising blog](https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/september-2026/less-busywork-more-growth-what-new-in-microsoft-advertising-this-september)). None was found for **X** either. Neither is worth building for UAE real estate.

## WhatsApp Cloud API is both the hottest inbox and the cheapest pager

Inbound WhatsApp leads arrive on the WABA's `messages` webhook. The request is signed with the same `X-Hub-Signature-256` HMAC as other Meta webhooks, and setup uses the same `hub.challenge` handshake ([Hookdeck](https://hookdeck.com/webhooks/platforms/guide-to-whatsapp-webhooks-features-and-best-practices)). Meta retries failed deliveries for up to about 7 days, so dedupe on the message ID. Production sending needs a System User token with `whatsapp_business_messaging` and `whatsapp_business_management`. Sources report that an **unverified business is capped at 250 business-initiated conversations a day and cannot send marketing templates**, and that verification takes about 2–7 business days ([Chatarmin](https://chatarmin.com/en/blog/whats-app-api-send-messages)). **WhatsApp Flows** turn the chat into a native form. The completed answers come back as an `nfm_reply` message whose `response_json` is a string to parse. The reply carries no Flow ID, so AI-DATA must correlate it through the `flow_token` it sets ([Meta Flows webhooks](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/flows/guides/flowswebhooks); [Meta: receive Flow response](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/flows/guides/receiveflowresponse)). The intended sequence is: CTWA ad, then the first message (with referral), then a Flow that collects name, area, budget, timeline and an explicit consent tick, then the lead record and the agent alert. Inside the 72-hour window, all of it is free.

Pricing changed on **1 Oct 2026**. Meta now charges for free-form service messages beyond a **free tier of 1,000 per business phone number per month** ([Meta pricing page](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing)). It also charges for utility templates sent inside an open 24-hour window ([Twilio notice](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/53100480177819-Notice-Changes-to-WhatsApp-s-Pricing-October-2026); [YCloud](https://www.ycloud.com/blog/whatsapp-api-message-pricing-update-effective-october-1-2026)). Service messages are billed at the market's utility rate, without volume tiers ([Wati](https://www.wati.io/en/blog/whatsapp-service-message-pricing/)). Sources disagree on whether a payment method had to be on file by 30 Sep 2026 ([getmanifest](https://blog.getmanifest.ai/whatsapp-service-message-pricing-october-2026/)). Meta's official UAE rate card could not be read, and the third-party figures conflict:

The price change has a design consequence. Inside the window, **reply to leads with free-form or interactive messages, not templates**. Agent alerts as utility templates cost roughly $16–18 per 1,000. A cheaper pattern is for each agent to tap "I'm on shift" at the start of the day, which opens a 24-hour window. Alerts then go out as interactive service messages with **Accept / Pass** buttons, and the button webhook measures the response SLA. That pattern is an inference; no source addresses staff alerts directly. Agents still have to **opt in**, because Meta requires opt-in before business-initiated messages and the policy has no internal-user exemption ([Meta opt-in guide](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/getting-opt-in)). Meta also classifies templates by content. Since April 2025, a template submitted as utility but judged promotional is approved as **marketing**, at about three times the UAE price, rather than rejected ([YCloud](https://www.ycloud.com/blog/whatsapp-api-message-template-category-guidelines-update)). Messaging limits have been **shared across the whole business portfolio since 7 Oct 2025** ([Meta messaging limits](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messaging-limits)). As a result, blocks provoked by lead-facing marketing can throttle internal alerts too.

Going direct to the Cloud API carries no platform fee. Business Solution Providers (BSPs) add either a flat licence (360dialog, about €49/month per number with no per-message markup) or a markup (Twilio, about $0.005 per message). The two models break even at about 10,000 messages a month ([EZContact](https://ezcontact.ai/en/blog/whatsapp-api-pricing-comparison-meta-twilio-360dialog-ezcontact/); [Telnyx](https://telnyx.com/resources/whatsapp-business-api-cost)). Wati adds roughly 20% on templates on top of $49–299 monthly plans ([eesel.ai](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/wati-pricing)). For AI-DATA's own number, direct Cloud API is cheapest. A BSP or Tech Provider Embedded Signup only matters when onboarding agencies' own numbers. Real estate is a permitted vertical ([Twilio allowed use cases](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039737793-What-use-cases-and-businesses-are-allowed-to-use-WhatsApp-on-Twilio)). **WhatsApp voice calling remains restricted in the UAE** under TDRA's VoIP rules ([Qontak](https://qontak.com/en/blog/whatsapp-ban-in-dubai/); [thebitjoy](https://thebitjoy.com/blogs/blog/does-whatsapp-work-in-dubai)), so "call the lead now" has to go over ordinary licensed telephony.

## Portal leads stay inside the agency that bought them

**Property Finder** is the only UAE portal with documented real-time lead delivery. Its partner-gated **Enterprise API** has a `GET /v1/leads` endpoint (scope `leads:read`) and **`lead.created`, `lead.updated` and `lead.assigned` webhooks**. An API key and secret are exchanged for a JWT that "currently expires in 30 minutes", and the keys are generated in PF Expert under Developer Resources ([PF Enterprise API docs](https://api-docs.propertyfinder.net/enterprise-api/index.html)). Those docs are password-protected, and only fragments were visible. The webhook payload schema, how webhooks are registered, the `X-Signature` HMAC details and the rate limits all remain unknown. The docs contradict themselves on whether off-plan **project leads** fire `lead.created` at all, so polling is needed to catch them. Third-party software reaches PF through the **Property Finder Developer Network (PFDN)**, which has sandboxes and requires accepting PF's API Access Terms ([PFDN](https://pfdn.propertyfinder.com/); [PFDN Integration Guide](https://pfdn.propertyfinder.com/integration-guide)). Sources disagree on access. One says an agency requests its own key ([Codingclave](https://codingclave.com/uae/services/property-finder-bayut-crm-integration)); another says the API is "only for software partners it approves… not for brokers directly" ([Cotlet.dev](https://cotlet.dev/integrations/property-finder-api-mcp-server)). PF's CRM-provider terms set a **12-month term payable in full in advance**, but the amount is not public ([PF CRM provider terms](https://www.propertyfinder.com/terms-conditions-for-crm-providers/)). WhatsApp leads are recorded only if the agency has **WhatsApp Insights or WhatsApp Enterprise** enabled ([PF Help](https://propertyfinderae1703058205.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/20199938398866)).

**Bayut and dubizzle** share one advertiser platform and publish **no developer documentation**. Integrators describe a "Push API Bearer token" generated in Bayut Pro, with one token covering both networks ([Ruby CRM](https://www.rubycrm.ai/integrations/bayut)). Others say the API key comes from Bayut support ([HelloLeads](https://helloleads.io/integrations/bayut-integration/)). Vendor accounts point to call and WhatsApp leads being pushed and email enquiries being pulled ([PropBridge](https://www.bitrix24.com/apps/app/vortexweb.property_leads/); [Codingclave](https://codingclave.com/uae/services/property-finder-bayut-crm-integration)). Both portals run a partner model rather than a self-serve developer portal ([Groovyweb](https://www.groovyweb.co/blog/property-finder-bayut-api-integration-architecture)). **Houza**, a brokerage-consortium portal launched in 2020, only documents a Propertybase partnership ([Online Marketplaces](https://www.onlinemarketplaces.com/articles/new-emirati-portal-houza-partners-with-propertybase-crm/)). Its leads realistically arrive by email. Parsing an agency's own forwarded notification emails is accepted practice, but vendors call it the older, non-real-time route ([PropCRM](https://propcrm.ae/blog/property-portal-integration-guide-uae)). Scraper "APIs" return agent listings, not buyer enquiries, and breach portal terms ([dubizzle Terms](https://uae.dubizzle.com/m/terms/); [Apify](https://apify.com/skyline_scrapers/uae-dubai-property-real-estate-leads-scraper-propertyfinder)).

The contracts matter more than the APIs. Property Finder's terms say three things ([PF Terms AE](https://www.propertyfinder.com/ae/terms-and-conditions/)). First, **"Property Finder is the data controller, and the User is the data processor"** for lead data. Second, agencies must label every PF lead as received from Property Finder, **"visible to all agents"**. Third, agencies **"shall not combine, mix or associate a lead received from Property Finder with or in a pool of leads"** from other platforms. The same terms hold the agency responsible for any CRM or integrator using the API on its behalf. Bayut's terms let service providers process personal data only under Bayut's instructions ([Bayut Terms](https://www.bayut.com/terms.html)). The Bayut Pro advertiser contract itself is not public. Together these rule out any portal lead entering AI-DATA's cross-agency pool. That reading is an inference: no explicit "no resale" clause was retrieved, but the controller, processor and no-pooling clauses leave little room.

## First-party forms, chat and calls are the clean feed for the Hot pool

The existing `POST /api/public/inquiry` with site keys, the allowed-origin check, the honeypot and required consent is the right core. Two parts of `server/hot.js` will not hold up on serverless. Its rate limiter is an in-memory `Map`, which each function instance keeps separately. And consent is captured as a yes/no flag plus a notice-version label, not as an event with the exact wording shown. For bot protection, **Cloudflare Turnstile** is reported as free and unlimited, but capped at 20 widgets per account ([Prosopo](https://prosopo.io/tools/cloudflare-turnstile-pricing/)). One widget can list several hostnames, which works around the cap. reCAPTCHA is free only for **10,000 assessments a month, pooled across the whole Google Cloud organisation** ([phpcaptcha.org](https://phpcaptcha.org/recaptcha-pricing/)). Phone OTP before marking a lead "verified hot" is attractive, but Twilio does not support alphanumeric sender IDs in the UAE ([Twilio UAE guidelines](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/guidelines/ae/regulatory)), so a local aggregator would be needed.

**Live chat** is cheap to wire up. Tawk.to is free and sends **chat-start, chat-end and transcript webhooks signed with HMAC-SHA1** ([tawk.to developer docs](https://developer.tawk.to/webhooks/)). By contrast, Tidio reserves webhooks for its Plus and Premium plans ([Tidio developer docs](https://developers.tidio.com/docs/webhooks-introduction)), and Intercom's Fin AI agent costs **$0.99 per resolution** ([Robylon](https://www.robylon.ai/blog/intercom-pricing-breakdown-2026)). For a lead platform, an in-house LLM qualifier behind chat and WhatsApp avoids a per-lead charge.

**Call tracking** is the most constrained channel. Twilio's UAE numbers need a Letter of Authorization to provision. Its geographic numbers are **inbound-only, with outbound calls "strictly prohibited"**, its toll-free +971 800 numbers **cannot be resold** and must terminate in the UAE, and two-way SMS is not supported ([Twilio UAE regulatory](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/guidelines/ae/regulatory); [Twilio UAE voice](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/guidelines/ae/voice)). Other providers require a UAE trade licence and an active e& or du corporate line ([AVOXI](https://support.avoxi.com/phone-number-requirements/document-required-trade-license-uae-did)). Activation can take weeks, and mobile-range numbers are not available ([CloudTalk](https://help.cloudtalk.io/en/articles/11397627-getting-local-numbers-and-calling-in-the-united-arab-emirates)). **CallRail does not serve the UAE** ([CallRail](https://support.callrail.com/hc/en-us/articles/5812465302925-What-countries-does-CallRail-serve)), and Telnyx users report outbound UAE calls blocked ([Retell community](https://community.retellai.com/t/uae-outbound-calls-blocked-with-telnyx-byot-call-country-not-supported-ae/3597)). Recording a call without consent is a criminal risk even for a participant. Law-firm commentary cites Cybercrime Decree-Law 34/2021 Art. 44: at least six months' imprisonment and/or AED 150,000–500,000 ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/privacy-protection/1805484/can-you-record-a-call-in-the-uae-and-use-it-in-court)). So a tracking line should either play an Arabic/English recording announcement before connecting, or record nothing and capture only caller ID, time and missed or answered status. That metadata is still enough to fire a missed-call hot-lead alert.

## Only AI-DATA's own consented leads may enter the shared Hot pool

The Hot pool works as a business on one condition: AI-DATA itself captured the enquiry, as controller, with consent that discloses where the details will go. The PDPL needs consent that is **specific, provable and withdrawable** ([Gowling WLG](https://gowlingwlg.com/en/insights-resources/articles/2022/uae-federal-decree-law-on-personal-data-protection/)). A lead consented "to be contacted by brokerage Y" cannot lawfully be passed to brokerage Z. Property Finder's terms forbid pooling its leads outright ([PF Terms AE](https://www.propertyfinder.com/ae/terms-and-conditions/)). The pool's form, ad-disclaimer and Flow wording should therefore read something like: *"AI-DATA will share my details with one licensed partner brokerage, who will contact me by phone or WhatsApp about this enquiry; my data may be stored outside the UAE."* The current exclusive, one-agency allocation in `allocate()` matches that promise. Selling one pool lead to several agencies would need different wording. A second constraint applies to AI-DATA's own ads. Any Dubai real-estate advertisement needs a **Trakheesi** permit ([DLD](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/news-media/dld-fines-10-real-estate-companies-and-warns-another-30-for-not-adhering-to-advertising-requirements/)). Pool-generating ads should therefore either advertise the matching service rather than specific listings, or run in partnership with a licensed brokerage that holds the permit. Whether a non-broker lead platform needs its own RERA licence was not established.

Today's `docs/HOT_LEADS.md` tells Zapier and Make users to send `"consent_contact": true` as a constant, including with a Hot pool key. That should change. The flag should map the platform's actual disclaimer response, and ad leads from an agency's own account should only ever use an agency key.

## Middleware buys reach for unbuilt connectors, not speed

Middleware is not the latency problem it is often assumed to be. **Zapier's triggers for Facebook Lead Ads, LinkedIn, TikTok and Snapchat are all instant (push)** ([Zapier Facebook Lead Ads](https://zapier.com/apps/facebook-conversions/integrations/facebook-lead-ads); [Zapier LinkedIn](https://zapier.com/apps/linkedin-ads/integrations/webhook/62333/post-new-linkedin-lead-gen-form-responses-to-webhooks); [Zapier TikTok](https://zapier.com/apps/tiktok-lead-generation/integrations/webhook/749293/trigger-webhooks-post-requests-for-new-tiktok-lead-generation-leads); [Zapier Snapchat](https://zapier.com/apps/snapchat-lead-generation/integrations/webhook/1783253/post-new-snapchat-lead-ads-to-webhooks-by-zapier-as-soon-as-leads-are-created)). Polling intervals of 15, 2 and 1 minutes on the Free, Professional and Team plans affect only non-webhook triggers ([Zapier help](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496244568589-How-Zap-triggers-work)). The real costs lie elsewhere. Pricing is per task, so it climbs with volume. Each tool adds a third-party processor holding UAE personal data, which counts as a cross-border transfer. Fields can be silently dropped in transit. Zaps built by each agency fail without anyone noticing. And consent flags tend to be hard-coded rather than mapped. Meta still needs Leads Access granted to Zapier. n8n's Facebook trigger shares the one-webhook-per-Meta-app limit, so test and production URLs overwrite each other ([n8n docs](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/trigger-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.facebookleadadstrigger/)).

## UAE law makes the callback, not the capture, the riskiest step

The **federal PDPL** (Decree-Law 45/2021) has been in force since 2 January 2022. As of October 2026, however, neither its Executive Regulations nor the Cabinet decision on penalties could be found on the official legislation portal ([Kayrouz & Associates](https://www.kayrouzandassociates.com/insights/cross-border-data-transfers-under-uae-law-in-2026); [BusinessDubai](https://www.businessdubai.ae/blogs/uae-pdpl-data-protection)). The UAE Data Office was folded into a new **Federal Authority for Artificial Intelligence and Data on 14 June 2026** ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/uae-creates-federal-authority-for-artificial-intelligence-and-data-1.500573822); [Al Tamimi](https://www.tamimi.com/law_update_articles/uae-establishes-new-federal-authority-for-ai-and-data-a-significant-step-in-the-evolution-of-digital-regulation/)). The often-quoted "AED 50,000–5 million" fines trace to no primary instrument ([Securiti](https://securiti.ai/uae-personal-data-protection-law/)). Enforcement risk is low today and likely to rise in 2027. Cross-border transfer needs adequacy, contractual safeguards or explicit consent, and **no adequacy list exists** ([Middle East Briefing](https://www.middleeastbriefing.com/news/uae-data-protection-obligations-and-cross-border-data-transfer-for-businesses/)). Any database outside the UAE therefore calls for a cross-border clause in the consent wording. Entities incorporated in DIFC or ADGM fall under their own regimes ([UAE Expert Hub](https://www.uaeexperthub.com/uae-data-protection-law-pdpl/)).

The **telemarketing regime is actively enforced.** Cabinet Resolutions 56 and 57 of 2024 took effect on **27 Aug 2024** and cover calls, SMS and marketing via social-media and messaging apps, including by free-zone companies ([Clyde & Co](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2024/07/uae-tightens-telemarketing-regulations-what-you-ne); [Ministry of Economy](https://www.moet.gov.ae/en/-/ministry-of-economy-and-telecommunications-and-digital-government-regulatory-authority-review-regulatory-legislations-in-organizing-operational-mechanisms-for-telemarketing-companies-in-the-uae-and-enhancing-consumer-protection-in-line-with-best-practices)). Companies need prior approval and must call from local numbers registered under their trade licence, never from personal numbers. Calls are allowed only between **9:00am and 6:00pm**. A refusal ends all further calls, and unanswered calls are capped at **one attempt a day and two a week**. Every marketing call must be recorded with notice at the start, and DNCR numbers must be suppressed. Fines reportedly reach AED 150,000 for calling DNCR numbers ([Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/new-telemarketing-rules-in-uae-full-list-of-fines-explained-as-laws-will-go-into-effect-from-august); [Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/developments/thought-leadership/new-telemarketing-rules-and-regulations-in-the-uae-a-comprehensive-guide-to-fines-and-enforcement/)). Over 2,000 people have been fined and banned for using personal numbers ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/crime/uae-fines-and-bans-over-2000-for-misusing-personal-numbers-in-telemarketing-1.1727928576834)). RERA separately fined a brokerage AED 50,000 and suspended nine brokers for cold calling ([DLD](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/news-media/rera-fines-real-estate-brokerage-office-aed-50-000-for-cold-calling-suspends-nine-brokers)). **No source found an exemption for calls returning a person's own enquiry.** A vendor even claims DNCR registration overrides earlier consent, which is unverified ([YuVerse](https://www.yuverse.ai/resources/posts/dncr-do-not-call-registry-uae)). Promotional SMS needs an **"AD-" sender prefix**, sender IDs registered separately with e& and du ([Telerivet](https://www.telerivet.com/blog/uae-sms-compliance-tdra-ad-prefix)), and a consent list uploaded to TDRA's Consent Management System ([D7 Networks](https://d7networks.com/blog/the-regulations-for-sending-sms-in-uae/)). Consumers can block a sender by texting 7726 ([TDRA FAQs](https://tdra.gov.ae/en/FAQs)). On the advertising side, every Dubai property ad, social media included, needs a **Trakheesi** permit with a **Madmoun** QR code (required since 24 Apr 2023) ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/property/dubai-land-department-launches-madmoun-service-for-real-estate-ad-verification-1.95202094)). Abu Dhabi has run its own Madhmoun permit through DARI since about July 2025 ([Driven Properties](https://www.drivenproperties.com/blog/adrec-abu-dhabi-real-estate-centre-guide)). A single source reports a federal **Media Council permit number on paid promotional posts from 1 Feb 2026** ([HiDubai](https://focus.hidubai.com/a-guide-to-the-new-uae-media-council-digital-advertising-permit/)).

## Speed-to-lead rewards seconds, but calls must wait for 9am

The evidence for responding fast is older and thinner than vendor marketing suggests, but it all points one way. The foundational audit is Oldroyd, McElheran and Elkington's **"The Short Life of Online Sales Leads"** (HBR, 2011) ([BYU ScholarsArchive](https://scholarsarchive.byu.edu/facpub/9711)). It is commonly summarised as finding that firms contacting a lead within an hour were about **7× as likely to qualify it**, and that **23% never responded** ([Vivocha summary](https://blog.vivocha.com/blog/short-life-online-sales-leads)). These figures were not checked against the original. The often-quoted **"21× more likely within 5 minutes than 30"** comes from a separate 2007 MIT/InsideSales study ([Perspective AI](https://getperspective.ai/blog/lead-response-time-2026-benchmarks-and-what-actually-converts)). The 2026 vendor benchmarks are inconsistent but sobering. They report a **42-hour median** first response (Aloware) ([Aloware](https://aloware.com/blog/lead-response-time-benchmarks)), a 1h42m median across 28,400 B2B leads ([Visionary Marketing](https://visionary-marketing.co.uk/blog/lead-response-time-statistics-2026)), and an average of more than 29 hours with **63% never responding** ([Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/s/average-lead-response-time)). Firms with a defined response SLA are about twice as likely to answer within 15 minutes, **54.9% versus 29.5%** ([Blazeo via TechDash](https://techdash.in/blazeo-unveils-2026-speed-to-lead-benchmark-report-revealing-businesses-are-failing-their-own-response-time-standards/)). Popular figures such as "917 minutes" and "391%" cannot be traced to a source ([LeadHaste](https://leadhaste.com/blog/time-to-first-response-benchmarks-2026)), and no UAE study exists.

In the UAE, speed-to-lead has to mean **speed-to-acknowledge plus a lawful, scheduled call**. When a lead arrives, the assigned agent is alerted within seconds. The lead gets an immediate, transactional acknowledgement on the channel they used: a free-form WhatsApp reply inside the 24-hour window, or email. A call is permitted at once if it is between 9am and 6pm GST and the number passes the DNCR and suppression checks; otherwise it is queued for 9am. Whether the 9am–6pm limit also covers WhatsApp and SMS is unclear, so keep anything promotional out of after-hours messages. The existing `first_contact_sla_minutes_hot` field and round-robin assignment in `createHotLead` already model the SLA. What is missing is delivery. Today an alert is just a row in the `notification` table, which an agent only sees by opening the app.

Alerts should go out over four channels in parallel, with escalation. **In-app realtime** uses a managed pub/sub service on a per-agent private channel. Ably's free tier gives about 6M messages a month and 200 concurrent connections ([BudgetForge](https://www.budgetforge.dev/tools/ably-pricing-2026)). Pusher's sandbox gives about 200K messages a day ([BuildMVPFast](https://www.buildmvpfast.com/tools/api-pricing-estimator/pusher-channels)). Holding connections open from Vercel functions does not work well, because they end at 300 seconds on Hobby and 800 on Pro ([Vercel limits](https://vercel.com/docs/limits)). **Web Push** uses VAPID keys and a service worker, and reaches agents when the tab is closed. On iPhone, it works only after the agent **adds the app to the Home Screen on iOS 16.4+**, and permission must be requested from a tap ([WebKit](https://webkit.org/blog/13878/web-push-for-web-apps-on-ios-and-ipados/)). If the agent denies it, they must remove and re-add the app before they can be asked again ([Pushpad](https://pushpad.xyz/blog/ios-special-requirements-for-web-push-notifications)). **WhatsApp** to opted-in agents carries Accept / Pass buttons. **SMS** is the fallback, sent from a registered transactional sender ID. Internal alerts are not consumer marketing, so the "AD-" prefix should not apply (an inference). If nobody accepts within N minutes, the lead re-alerts and is reassigned. The Blazeo data on SLAs is the strongest argument for making that timer explicit and visible.

## Persistent storage and a queue must precede every connector

The current backend opens `node:sqlite` `DatabaseSync` on a file in `/tmp`. On Vercel that file belongs to one function instance and disappears whenever the instance is recycled. Pool leads, consent evidence and the `webhook_receipt` dedupe table can all be lost, and two concurrent instances see different data. **This is the first thing to fix.** No major serverless Postgres or SQLite provider confirmed a UAE region, so the choice is between three trade-offs. **Neon Postgres in Frankfurt (`fra1`)** is the safest default, since Neon lists Frankfurt as its nearest region ([Neon regions](https://neon.tech/docs/introduction/regions)), though moving from SQLite needs dialect changes. **Supabase in Mumbai (`bom1`)** adds Supabase Realtime for in-app updates ([Supabase regions](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/regions)). **Turso/libSQL** needs the smallest code change because it speaks SQLite, but Supabase announced its acquisition of Turso in October 2026, which leaves Turso's roadmap uncertain ([Turso blog](https://turso.tech/blog/turso-is-joining-supabase)). Whichever is chosen, call `attachDatabasePool` from `@vercel/functions` ([Vercel docs](https://vercel.com/docs/functions/functions-api-reference/vercel-functions-package)) and pin functions to the database's region ([Vercel docs](https://vercel.com/docs/functions/debug-slow-functions)). Vercel's Dubai region **`dxb1`** runs on AWS `me-central-1` ([Vercel changelog](https://vercel.com/changelog/introducing-the-dubai-vercel-region-dxb1)), but it is not a safe sole region. It suffered a multi-AZ outage in March 2026 ([isdown](https://isdown.app/status/vercel/incidents/545104-dubai-region-dxb1-is-unavailable)) and was reported "currently unavailable" on 2 Oct 2026 ([GitHub PR](https://github.com/xpexellent-dotcom/msrc-2027/pull/13)). Failover across regions is Enterprise-only ([Vercel region docs](https://vercel.com/docs/functions/configuring-functions/region)). AI-DATA is commercial, and **Hobby is restricted to non-commercial use** ([Vercel Hobby](https://vercel.com/docs/plans/hobby)), so the project belongs on Pro.

Every source should follow the same ingest path:

```
Source ──HTTPS POST──▶ https://<custom-domain>/api/webhooks/<source>/<tenant>
  ingest fn (fra1): raw body → verify signature/key → INSERT inbound_events ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING
                    → enqueue {event_id} with idempotencyKey = source:external_id → 200
  queue (Vercel Queues or QStash, retries) ──▶ worker:
        fetch full lead if thin (Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok) → normalise (+971 E.164, Arabic digits)
        → consent check → route by source policy (Hot pool vs one agency CRM) → lead + task
        → fan-out: Ably/Pusher · Web Push · WhatsApp to agent · SMS fallback · email
  Vercel Cron (5–15 min): backfill pollers + per-source "no leads in N hours" health checks
```

**Vercel Queues** is in public beta. It costs from $0.60 per million operations and supports `idempotencyKey` deduplication within the retention window, which defaults to 24 hours and can be set up to 7 days. Queues are regional ([Vercel changelog](https://vercel.com/changelog/vercel-queues-now-in-public-beta); [Vercel Queues SDK](https://vercel.com/docs/queues/sdk)). QStash (about 1,000 messages a day free) and Inngest (about 50,000 runs a month free) are mature alternatives ([APIScout](https://apiscout.dev/guides/upstash-qstash-vs-inngest-vs-aws-sqs-2026); [BuildMVPFast](https://www.buildmvpfast.com/alternatives/inngest)). The in-memory rate limiter should give way to `checkRateLimit` from `@vercel/firewall` ([Vercel WAF SDK](https://vercel.com/docs/vercel-firewall/vercel-waf/rate-limiting-sdk)). Backfill crons are authenticated with `CRON_SECRET` ([Vercel cron docs](https://vercel.com/docs/cron-jobs/manage-cron-jobs)). Register webhooks against the stable production domain, ideally a custom one. Never use per-deployment URLs, which Standard Deployment Protection blocks ([Vercel Authentication](https://vercel.com/docs/deployment-protection/methods-to-protect-deployments/vercel-authentication)). Two smaller changes follow. The strict `connect-src 'self'` in `vercel.json`'s Content-Security-Policy must be widened for the realtime provider. Tenant OAuth and Page tokens must be encrypted at rest, with token errors (e.g. Meta #190) mapped to a "Reconnect" banner.

## Conclusion

Building AI-DATA's live-lead engine is mostly an operations and legal problem, not an engineering one. The webhooks are well documented, free and fast. What decides success is whether tokens stay valid, approvals land, pollers catch what webhooks drop, and every lead carries the provenance that determines where it may legally go. That shifts the platform's real asset from connectors to **policy**. Each source needs a recorded rule that answers two questions: is it pool-eligible, and what consent did the person actually give. The software enforces the answer at routing time. One consequence follows for the business model. Portal integrations are worth building as a feature that keeps agencies subscribed, but they can never supply the Hot pool. Only AI-DATA's own ad spend, WhatsApp number and websites can, so the pool's margin depends on AI-DATA's first-party acquisition cost.

The UAE adds one twist that a fast-response product can turn into an advantage. Resolution 56 forbids marketing calls outside 9am–6pm and caps attempts, while response-time research rewards contact within minutes. A platform that alerts the agent within seconds, acknowledges the lead immediately on WhatsApp, and schedules the first lawful call for the earliest permitted slot can be both fast and compliant. With the October 2026 WhatsApp pricing change, that acknowledgement costs almost nothing when sent as a free-form reply.
